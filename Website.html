How runs are classified

extinct
No live cells left at the end.
saturated
More than 85% of cells alive over the last 60 steps.
stable
Population exactly constant over the last 60 steps.
periodic
Population exactly repeats with a period of 20 or less.
active
Anything else: gliders in transit, growth fronts, disordered churn.
